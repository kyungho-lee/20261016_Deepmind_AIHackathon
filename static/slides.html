1. Problem & Impact

Why 2D Flat Navigation & Unverified LLMs Fail in Flash Floods

02 / 07

1. Bowl-Shaped Gangnam Basin

Seoul's Gangnam Station intersection forms a steep topographical bowl (central underpass at 13.5m surrounded by 38.5m–41.2m hills).

During cloudbursts (water_level = 2.0m, flood plane z = 18.5m), stormwater rapidly submerges the central basin.

2. Deadly 2D Shortest Paths

Standard 2D map routers optimize strictly for horizontal distance (850m):
GN_01 → GN_02 → GN_06 → GN_08

This leads citizens directly into the submerged Gangnam Underpass (GN_02, z = 13.5m, water = 0.45m).

3. The GeoRescue 3D Solution

Combines Gemini 4 Argon (1M Context) with an isolated Python NetworkX A* Sandbox Verifier.

Guarantees every evacuation node satisfies z_elevation > 18.5m and water_level_m ≤ 0.15m before rendering on a 3D WebGL Digital Twin.
